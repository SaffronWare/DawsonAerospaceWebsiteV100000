01. Overview

Who We Are

The Dawson Aerospace Club (DAC) is an interdisciplinary team of collegiate engineers, builders, and researchers dedicated to practical aerospace and mechanical engineering.

We bridge theoretical coursework with hands-on technical execution—enabling students to design flight control avionics, machine precision drivetrains, and launch high-altitude rocket payloads.

02. Engineering Divisions

Active Projects

01. Avionics & Unmanned Systems

Focused on embedded electronics, custom flight controller firmware, and fixed-wing UAV restoration—highlighted by our Janice RC plane platform.

02. Formula SAE Engineering

Designing high-performance open-wheel racecar components, space-frame structural analysis, and custom powertrain integration.

03. Amateur Rocketry

High-power rocketry certification builds (CAR Level 1 & 2), solid motor casing design, and parachute deployment recovery systems.

04. Launch Canada

Intercollegiate rocketry challenge development—engineering high-altitude sounding rockets for national competition.
