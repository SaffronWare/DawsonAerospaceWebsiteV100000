Avionics

Flight computers, sensors, telemetry, and the embedded software and control systems that fly on everything the club builds.

DIV / AV
Current Project

Janice — RC Plane Restoration

The first official project of the Dawson Aerospace Club is the restoration of our RC airplane: Janice. Janice was a gas-powered, single-cylinder plane. We decided the best way to get her flying again was to fully electrify her. We applied to The Dawson Foundation and got a SEEDS grant to allow us to purchase the parts we needed. A lot had to go into this project, whether it was redesigning the nose for the new motor, gutting the interior to allow for new hardware or creating an on-board flight controller from scratch.

Formula FRC

Formula SAE-style vehicle engineering and FIRST Robotics Competition robots — mechanical design, CAD, manufacturing, and controls.

DIV / FRC
Current Project

FRC — FIRST Robotics Competition

The FIRST Robotics Competition is one of the largest STEM competitions in the world. It provides a great opportunity for new members of the team to gain some experience in engineering. We intend to have the FRC branch of the club be run entirely by first years, with supervision from some of our own FRC veterans.

Future Project

FSAE

The world is electrifying fast; nowhere is that more apparent than the automobile industry. 20 years ago, electric cars were a distant idea akin to flying cars; now they are approaching the norm. The Dawson Aerospace Club intends to participate in the FSAE competition next year. Among us are many motivated mechanical engineering techs. which have found a practical way to use what they've learnt.

Rocketry

Airframe design, aerodynamics, recovery systems, and simulation, plus propulsion-related research within safe, legal, educational limits.

DIV / RKT
Current Project

Amateur Rocketry Program

The DAC serves as the primary hub for amateur rocketry at Dawson College. Whether you're interested in getting your CAR/ACF certification, building your own custom rocket motors or just generally interested in the field, this is the place for you. As the barrier to space continues to break down, Canada must have a part in this new industry. Our members continue to learn and gain experience in preparation for Launch Canada Junior next year.

Future Project

Launch Canada

Launch Canada is the largest student rocketry competition in Canada, and we intend to be the first cegep students to compete in it. We are building an experienced team to compete in Launch Canada Junior, the high school-level competition, as a stepping stone to a university-level rocketry program.
